How to use: Copy a YouTube livestream link, paste it into a slot, and tap Load. Choose 1, 2, or 4 streams at the top. Tap the player's own controls to start playback and adjust volume.
Tips: Start with 2 streams on a phone. Four simultaneous HD streams may use a lot of data, RAM and battery. Some videos may refuse embedding because of the channel owner's settings; those streams must be watched on YouTube instead.
